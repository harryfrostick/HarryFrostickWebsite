About me

Harry
Frostick.

Working at the intersection of craft, curiosity, and genuine storytelling.

With 7 years of experience across freelance, in-house, and agency environments, I have had the privilege of creating motion and video work for some of the world's most recognised commercial brands. I bring the same level of care to a five-second social loop as I do to a full brand campaign, because the details always matter.

Creating for the world's biggest brands

AI-Creative

AI tools enhance creativity, accelerate ideation, and speed up workflow so that the emphasis can be placed where the artist chooses.

I've begun identifying with the term 'creative technologist' as I blend more skills in motion design with technical problem-solving. I use AI to create innovative workflows so that I can focus on the areas which I care about most like the way in which the subject moves, or story I'm trying to tell. Motion design already taught me to become a generalist; AI becomes yet another string to my bow.
